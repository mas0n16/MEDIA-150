With 'AI slop' distorting our reality, the world is sleepwalking into disaster

Reading 2 Summary


Alternative intelligence is changing our perception of what is real. With AI making its way into politics and spreading like wildfire across social media platforms, it’s becoming more and more dangerous to be in a world where we constantly have to guess what is real and what is not. The article “With ‘AI Slop’ distorting our reality, the world is sleepwalking into disaster” by Nesrine Malik states that there are two parallel image channels that dominate our daily visual consumption. One contains real pictures and footage of the world as it is with politics, sports, news and entertainment. The other is AI slop as she calls it—low-quality content with minimal human input. When the latter was on the rise, it was much easier to grasp which was which, but as the technology builds upon itself, it is becoming increasingly difficult to refrain our minds from accepting what seems real. The result of this content is not just a blurring of reality, but a distortion of it. A new genre of this AI slop is rightwing political fantasy as she calls it. “There entire YouTube videos of made-up scenarios in which Trump officials prevail against liberal forces”. Trump has shared AI content of him and Netanyahu sipping cocktails in a ‘Trump Gaza' laid back on land that they destroyed to build resorts. To have this type of behavior facilitated by the sitting President is something we have never experienced before, and makes you wonder if there is a line to the lengths this kind of content will go.
