Creative Statement

My name is Mason Riehm and I am a Media Studies student at Hunter College. I have a passion for creating content with effective and unique communication styles. I like to utilize my knowledge of aesthetics to create media that portrays a meaningful message and invokes thoughts and connections between the art and the viewers' personal experiences.

Through my studies, I will widen my understanding of media, culture, and society, along with how they influence each other in the bigger picture. I am mostly interested in the entertainment industry, specifically creative expression and marketing in the music industry. I enjoy expressing myself and my experiences through music and media including video, photo, and a curation of both. I am also passionate about advocacy for human rights and politics for the people — it is important to me to use my creativity to inspire others to be better.

I have experience in experiential marketing, content creation, and creating memorable experiences for audiences. I am excited to learn new skills and techniques that will help me grow as a professional in media. I believe media can be a powerful tool for social change, and I look forward to discovering the intersections between my interests and my studies at Hunter College.
